About

Serene Library is a calm little corner of the internet for readers. Browse by genre, pick a book, and settle in.

The site uses soft sage greens to keep screens easy on the eyes at night, and there is a dark mode for late reading.

  • 30 stories in four genres: Romance, Fantasy, Thriller and Action, one chapter each
  • Show or hide each story's synopsis, and like the ones you enjoy
  • A reading progress bar on every story page
  • Works on phones, tablets, and desktops

Serene Library · Read slowly, dream softly.
