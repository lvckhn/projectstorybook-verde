The Friend Zone

By Abby Jimenez · Romance

Synopsis

Two close friends start to fall for each other, but one of them is hiding a secret that could keep them apart.

A Fender Bender in Burbank

Summary of chapter 1

Josh has just moved from South Dakota to California for a new job, and his morning is already going badly. His ex-girlfriend has refused to pay her share of a big appliance bill, the cost of living is draining his savings, and his car’s air conditioning is broken.

Stuck in Sunday-morning traffic in Burbank, he lightly bumps into the truck ahead of him. The driver, a small and very annoyed woman, pulls into a grocery store parking lot so they can inspect the damage. It turns out to be minor, and the two agree to skip insurance.

Josh is caught admiring her as she leans into her truck, and she tells him to make himself useful. It turns out she has spilled coffee all over her friend’s new truck, and he hands over his favorite gym shirt to help her clean it up. A small dog in a tiny outfit growls at him from the back seat.
