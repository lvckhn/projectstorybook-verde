The Hunter's Gonna Lay Low

By Baek Sam · Fantasy

Synopsis

A powerful hunter hides his strength and tries to live a quiet life, even as monsters keep slipping into his city.

The Leek in the Alley

Summary of chapter 1

On his way home from the convenience store, a quiet young man in a gray hoodie walks past a rift in the city where monsters are pouring out and hunters are fighting them. Taking a shortcut through an alley, he runs into a huge toad monster that has slipped through.

Annoyed, he takes out the one weapon he has on hand, a leek from his grocery bag, and knocks the toad out with a single blow. He then calls the authorities with a frightened voice, claiming he found the dead monster, and erases his footprints before slipping away.

Two hunters from the rift agency arrive and are baffled by how cleanly the monster was killed, with no trace of any ability. Meanwhile, the young man turns out to run a small hangover-soup restaurant, and goes about prepping for the dinner rush as if nothing had happened.
