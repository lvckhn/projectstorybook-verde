Into the Drowning Deep

By Mira Grant · Fantasy

Synopsis

A young marine biologist joins a risky expedition to find out what really happened to a missing ship, and to prove that the ocean still holds secrets.

Whale-Watching Season

Summary of chapter 1

On a July morning in Monterey, California, marine biologist Tory works as the guide on a whale-watching boat, pointing out sea lions, dolphins and whales to excited tourists. She loves the ocean and the awe on the visitors’ faces, but she is counting down the days until she returns to her graduate studies.

She recalls how her ex-boyfriend lectured her about wasting her summer, and how she has quietly been studying the way groups of tourists convince themselves they have seen things. She also reflects on how warming oceans are changing the lives of whales and other sea creatures.

When the boat reaches a pod of orcas, Tory goes off script and tells the passengers how badly orcas are treated in captivity. The first mate cuts her off and orders her to stick to the script, and she goes back to her scripted lines, while wishing the sea would reveal its secrets.
