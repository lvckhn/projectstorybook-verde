God of Ruin

By Rina Kent · Romance

Synopsis

A girl bent on revenge sneaks into a secret club’s masked party to take down the man she blames for her brother’s injury.

A Masked Party

Summary of chapter 1

Mia and her twin sister Maya sneak, masked, into a lavish party held by a secretive elite club on a rival college’s turf. Mia is there for revenge on Landon King, whom she blames for the kidnapping and injury of their older brother Niko.

When the lights dim, Mia is overcome by panic from a childhood trauma, and Maya steadies her. The twins then split up to check on the plan Mia set in motion the day before.

After crawling through an air vent to reach the control panel, Mia is shoved back into a bathroom by an unseen figure and comes face to face with Landon himself.
