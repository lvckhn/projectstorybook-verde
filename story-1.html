I'm in Love with the Villainess

By Inori · Romance

Synopsis

An overworked office worker is reincarnated as the heroine of her favorite otome game, and promptly falls for its villainess.

A Strange New Classroom

Summary of chapter 1

Rei, an overworked office employee in Japan whose only real joy is playing games, suddenly finds herself in a classroom full of foreign-looking students. A girl with blonde curls is scolding her for pushing her desk up against hers, and Rei slowly realizes she knows exactly who this is: Claire François, her favorite character from the otome game “Revolution”.

Rei works out that she has been reincarnated into the game as its heroine. Instead of being alarmed, she is delighted by how Claire reacts, blurting out that she is cute, and even asking Claire to keep bullying her.

Claire is flustered and confused, but Rei is already imagining a bright new life of admiring her, far away from the overtime she left behind.
