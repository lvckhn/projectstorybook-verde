The Hunting Party

By Lucy Foley · Thriller

Synopsis

A group of old friends celebrates New Year at a remote Scottish lodge, and by the end of the trip, one of them is dead.

Snow at the Lodge

Summary of chapter 1

The story opens in a remote Scottish lodge on January 2nd, with a snowstorm raging. Heather, who works at the lodge, sees the gamekeeper stagger toward her through the snow, pale with shock. He tells her he has found the missing guest, and that it is a body.

It then jumps back three days. Emma is on a train to the Scottish Highlands with a group of old university friends for their yearly New Year’s getaway, which she has planned in detail. She is the newest member of the group and is eager to finally feel accepted.

A seating mix-up leaves one friend, Katie, alone in another carriage, and the charming Miranda smooths it over. Emma notices the tensions and old closeness among the friends, and hopes the trip will make her belong.
