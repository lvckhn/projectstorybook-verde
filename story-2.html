Love on the Brain

By Ali Hazelwood · Romance

Synopsis

A gifted neuroscientist lands her dream NASA project, only to find that her co-lead is the academic rival she can’t stand.

An Unwelcome Co-Lead

Summary of chapter 1

Bee Königswasser, a young neuroscientist at the National Institutes of Health, has just been chosen to lead BLINK, a NASA-backed project to build performance-boosting technology for astronauts. After years of rejected grants and a stalled career, the news feels like a turning point, and she spends the weekend celebrating with friends, fresh purple hair and a first attempt at running.

The excitement fades when she learns who will co-lead the project with her: Levi Ward, a former grad-school colleague she is certain has always looked down on her and her work. She phones her free-spirited sister Reike, who finds the whole situation hilarious and teases Bee about avoiding closeness and dating.

While packing for three months in Houston, Bee recalls how the rivalry began, and tucks in the blue polka-dot dress she wore on the day she decided Levi couldn’t stand her.
