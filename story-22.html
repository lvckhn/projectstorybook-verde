The Day of the Jackal

By Frederick Forsyth · Thriller

Synopsis

A mysterious assassin is hired to kill the French president, and one detective races to stop him.

Ambush at Petit-Clamart

Summary of chapter 1

The book opens in Paris in August 1962, as President de Gaulle’s motorcade leaves the Elysée Palace. A group of right-wing extremists from the OAS, led by Colonel Bastien-Thiry, have planned an ambush on the road at Petit-Clamart to kill him because they believe he betrayed France by giving up Algeria.

Their plan goes wrong when Bastien-Thiry miscalculates when dusk will fall. The gunmen fire on the convoy, but de Gaulle’s driver keeps the damaged car moving, and the president escapes unhurt. The police then launch a huge manhunt and arrest most of the plotters.

The chapter goes on to describe how the French secret service’s hard-line Action Service hit back at the OAS, including the abduction of an OAS leader, Colonel Argoud. It ends with Bastien-Thiry’s execution, which sets off a new plot by the next OAS leader.
