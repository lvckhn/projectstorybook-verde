Final Girls

By Riley Sager · Thriller

Synopsis

The survivor of a massacre has built a quiet life, until news of another survivor’s death threatens to drag her past back.

A Text from Coop

Summary of chapter 1

The book opens with Quincy running barefoot and bleeding through dark woods at night, fleeing something she can no longer remember, and realizing that everyone else with her is dead.

Years later, Quincy is a baking blogger in Manhattan with a lawyer boyfriend, Jeff. She keeps her anxiety in check with pills and pepper spray. A text from Coop, the police officer who found her that night, brings her to a cafe for a meeting. She has no memory of about an hour of the massacre that made her a survivor, and has learned to live without the details.

Coop tells her that Lisa Milner, another survivor of a massacre who had mentored Quincy, has died. Quincy realizes there are now only two of them left.
