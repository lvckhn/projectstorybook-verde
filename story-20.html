Gone Girl

By Gillian Flynn · Thriller

Synopsis

On their anniversary, a man’s wife disappears, and as the search goes on, he becomes the main suspect.

The Day Of

Summary of chapter 1

On the morning of his fifth wedding anniversary, Nick Dunne wakes up thinking about his wife Amy and wondering what is going on inside her head. He explains how they moved from New York to his small Missouri hometown after both lost their jobs and his mother fell seriously ill, a move Amy resents.

Amy is downstairs making breakfast, in good spirits, but Nick is uneasy. He later heads to the bar he runs with his twin sister, bought with Amy’s money, and describes the town and its river. A feeling of being watched leaves him needing a drink.
